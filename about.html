Who we are

The best of the best in Kenyan living.

AfraViva Homes brings you a portfolio of artfully designed, fully furnished and serviced apartments — 1, 2 and 3 bedroom — across Nairobi and the Kenyan Coast, for stays from a single night to a long-term lease, at whatever stage each development is actually at.

The name is short for "African viva" — living, present tense. That's the standard we hold every listing to: real locations, real developers, real timelines, stated plainly rather than dressed up. Where a unit is ready, we say so and invite a viewing. Where a render is a render, we label it as one.

How we work

Three things we don't compromise on.

01

Honest staging

Every listing carries its real status — ready now, under construction with a target date, or coming soon. No property is dressed up as further along than it is.

02

Labelled renders

Where a unit isn't built yet, what you see is marked as an artist's impression. Finishes and layouts can shift during construction — we'd rather set that expectation early.

03

Direct access

Every property connects straight to a real enquiry — a form, a call, or WhatsApp. No gatekeeping, no forced sign-up walls before you can ask a basic question.
